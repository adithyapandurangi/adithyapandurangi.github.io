A cheaper way to grow microalgae.

We design LED-lit photobioreactors that run indoors, all year, on biogenic CO2.

Technology

  • Economic channel geometry

    Our own panel design. We share the details under NDA.

  • LED optimisation

    We match the light to the culture, so less power is wasted.

  • Closed loop

    The culture stays clean and controlled, whatever the weather.

  • Biogenic CO2

    We feed the reactor CO2 from fermentation that would otherwise be vented.
